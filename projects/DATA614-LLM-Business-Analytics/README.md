# From Text to Insight: How Large Language Models Are Transforming Business Analytics Automation

**Course:** DATA 614 – Advanced Data Analytics, University of Calgary (Winter 2026)
**Team:** Bhuvan Dubey, Gurmol Sohi, Bilal Naseem
**Type:** Group white paper / literature review (no code component — conceptual/industry-report format)

## Summary

This paper examines how Large Language Models (LLMs) are reshaping business analytics — automating tasks like report generation, customer service triage, document summarization, and forecasting support that traditionally required manual human effort on unstructured data (emails, tickets, contracts, chat logs).

It covers:
- **How LLMs work** in this context: transformer architectures, embeddings, fine-tuning, and in-context/few-shot learning.
- **Benefits:** speed/scale, reduced manual effort, better decision support, and democratized access via natural-language interfaces.
- **Risks and governance:** hallucination, data privacy/PII exposure, training-data bias, compute cost, and domain misalignment for general-purpose models.
- **Where the field is heading:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ground outputs in enterprise knowledge, domain-specific fine-tuned models, agentic multi-step workflows, and long-context models for multi-document reasoning.

## Why it's relevant

Written as a practitioner-facing "industry report" rather than a pure academic paper — the goal was to make the case for (and against) LLM adoption in a way that's useful to an analytics team actually deciding whether to build this into their workflow, not just a research summary.
